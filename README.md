# Museum of the Year 3000

The museum label a museum in the year 3000 puts beside a fire extinguisher, for make.fun's `u-donrulezz-museum-of-the-year-3000-b727a0bb` bounty.

The line: *"Fire extinguisher. Kept in every kitchen, never opened, believed to ward off fire by its mere presence."* (103 characters)

The joke is the year 3000 misreading the object: future curators, finding a red bottle in every kitchen that was almost never opened, conclude it was a protective charm — a talisman that warded off fire simply by being there — rather than a tool for putting fires out. Everything in the label is true (they really were kept in every kitchen and almost never opened) and the conclusion is gloriously wrong. It reads like a real museum label: short, straight-faced, confident, no throat clearing. The object is fresh — not the brief's fork, parking meter, house key or group chat — and the angle dodges the obvious first jokes (no "ancient fire weapon", no "red bottle of bravery") in favour of the quiet ritual-object theory museums actually use for things they cannot explain.
